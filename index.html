The problem

PPC eats your week. Or it eats your margin.

Most sellers end up stuck between two bad options.

  • Doing it yourself takes hours. Search term reports, bids, negatives, placements. Every week. It never ends.
  • An agency takes a cut and the control. You pay a monthly fee or a slice of spend, and still do not know why your ACoS moved.
  • Generic AI gives generic answers. Paste a report into a chatbot and you get vague advice that ignores your margins and your goals.
  • The basics slip. Wasted spend on irrelevant terms. Winners never moved to exact match. Bids nobody has touched in months.
There is a third option. Keep running your own PPC, with an AI that knows how your account works and does the hard part in minutes.

What the skills do

Everything your weekly PPC routine needs.

Each skill does one job well. Together they cover the whole account.

Analyse

Search term report analysis

Reads your report and sorts terms into winners, wasters and ones to watch. No more scrolling through thousands of rows.

Cut waste

Negative keywords

Finds the terms burning spend with no sales and gives you a ready-to-upload negative list, by campaign.

Optimise

Bid changes

Suggests bid moves based on your target ACoS and real performance. You see the reasoning before you change anything.

Organise

Campaign structure

Reviews how your campaigns are set up and plans a cleaner structure. Harvests winning terms into exact match.

Diagnose

ACoS and TACoS diagnosis

When numbers move, it tells you why. Spend, conversion, price, organic rank or seasonality, in plain English.

Report

Weekly reports

A short summary of what changed, what worked and what to do next. Ready to read in five minutes or share with a partner.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does it work with Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?

Yes. I build on whichever you prefer: a Claude Project, a custom GPT or a Gemini Gem. If you do not use any of them yet, I will help you pick one on the audit call.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can download a report from Seller Central and paste it into a chat, you can use the skills. I walk you through it and you get a short guide.

Is my data safe?

Your skills live in your own AI account, not mine. Your reports stay between you and your AI platform. I do not need your Seller Central login to build them. Check your platform's data settings to control whether your chats are used for training.

What if I already have an agency?

That is fine. Many sellers use the skills to check their agency's work and ask better questions. Some decide to bring PPC back in-house once they see how quick it is. Your call.

Is this a course?

No. There are no videos to watch or modules to finish. You get working AI skills built for your account, and support when you need it.

Will the AI change my campaigns on its own?

No. The skills recommend. You decide and make the changes. Nothing happens in your account without you.
